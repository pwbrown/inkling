# Credits

Third-party content bundled with Inkling, with its source and license (REQUIREMENTS §12).

## Word list

- **What:** English words in `src/content/en/words.json`, generated by `scripts/build-words.ts`.
- **Source:** [SCOWL](http://wordlist.aspell.net/) (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson. Size levels 10, 20, 35, 40 and 50 are used, via the [`wordlist-english`](https://github.com/jacksonrayhamilton/wordlist-english) npm package (MIT).
- **License:** the SCOWL license, which permits use and redistribution with its copyright notice. The full notice is in [`licenses/SCOWL.txt`](licenses/SCOWL.txt).
- **Changes:** filtered to lowercase a–z words of 2–12 letters, with offensive words removed using the blocklist below.

## Blocklist

- **What:** terms in `src/content/en/blocklist.json`, used to filter the word list and every generated string.
- **Source:** [List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words) (LDNOOBW), via the [`naughty-words`](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/naughty-words-js) npm package.
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Changes:** multi-word phrases removed, a few extra terms added, and an allowlist of harmless words the matching rules caught by mistake (both lists are in `scripts/build-words.ts`).

## Topic word lists (optional download)

- **What:** words downloaded on request in the Word lists screen. Nothing is bundled.
- **Source:** the [Datamuse API](https://www.datamuse.com/api/), a free word-finding service that needs no key.
- **Use:** requests only the `ml` (related words) query with the player's topic. Results are filtered to single lowercase words and run through the blocklist before use.
